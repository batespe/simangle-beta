What SimAngle does

SimAngle is a lightweight setup companion for sim-racing and desktop enthusiasts. It combines practical setup tools in one app so you can tune your driving position and display configuration quickly, without switching between multiple utilities.

  • Digital spirit level and angle finder using your device motion sensors
  • Guided measurement modes to help you hold and orient your device correctly
  • Field-of-view (FOV) calculator to support realistic in-game camera setup
  • Offline-first design with no account required for core functionality

Why we are running a beta

Testing on real devices and real setups helps us validate sensor behavior, calculation reliability, and usability before public release. Your feedback helps us find edge cases, improve clarity, and prioritize the most valuable fixes.

What to test

Please focus on everyday usage and let us know where things feel unclear, slow, or inaccurate. Practical feedback from your own rig and device is the most valuable.

  • Angle and spirit level responsiveness across different device orientations and surfaces
  • FOV calculations using your own setup dimensions and expected in-game results
  • Switching between tools and measurement modes during a normal setup session
  • Usability, readability, and any crashes or unexpected app behavior
